03 · AURA product fit

Where Etimad could create value in AURA

Etimad has the strongest potential for Saudi government and semi-government customers or an eligible financial-sector partner. The value is conditional on a narrow, approved product and user journey. It is not a broad employee-experience connector by default.

All AURA apps

  • Surface approved government contract or employee certificate data in a customer’s permitted workflow.
  • Reuse a governed connector framework across AURA apps only after Etimad authorizes the application, purpose, and user group.
  • Centralize access logs, customer-level configuration, and consent/authority evidence.

Employee Hub

  • Potential niche: government or semi-government employee deduction or salary-related service.
  • Potential financial partner journey: obtain a certificate for an employee credit inquiry, subject to the documented product and authority model.
  • Keep records and results restricted to the employee and authorized customer roles.

Not yet evidenced

  • General-purpose employee master data or HR self-service access.
  • Purchase request creation, approval, or status synchronization.
  • Broad public procurement search for all AURA customers.
  • Any right to pool, reuse, resell, or expose Etimad data across tenants.

05 · Access and onboarding

How production access is obtained

Etimad’s own onboarding information separates portal exploration and sandbox testing from production access. Production requires an account tied to an eligible entity, a subscription request for a specific product, and review.

1 · QualifyConfirm customer entity, sector, AURA role, end users, and intended data purpose.
2 · SelectChoose the exact API product and review its API details, integration guide, terms, and price plan.
3 · SubscribeSign in with the entity’s Etimad account; request the product subscription and await review/approval.
4 · TestCreate an application, use sandbox/sample data, and validate contract, errors, identities, and rate limits.
5 · Go liveObtain production credentials/URL and deploy only after customer, legal, security, and provider gates.

Access facts from Etimad

  • Portal and sandbox are available to developers; production requirements depend on Government or Business account status.
  • A subscription application is submitted for a specific product; the Etimad team reviews the request.
  • Application key and secret are generated after creating an application.
  • Product subscriptions are not automatically transferable across sectors or products.
  • Portal FAQ says production fees appear during subscription submission; some APIs may be available free without registration.

Commercial/legal clarification

  • Contracts Plus states its target audience as Financial and Banking Sector, FinTech.
  • Portal terms restrict use to the intended purpose and prohibit reselling or sharing without prior written permission.
  • Terms also contain a clause limiting Developer Portal resources to test applications. Clarify in writing how that clause interacts with the separately documented production subscription process.
  • Confirm the Arabic product terms where language versions differ; the Contracts Plus page says Arabic prevails in a conflict.
Credentials and tenant modelDo not place Etimad app secrets in a browser or a shared AURA-wide configuration. Confirm whether Etimad permits one AURA-operated app for multiple customer tenants or requires a separate application/subscription per customer. Store secrets server-side, isolate tenant credentials and entitlements, rotate keys, and restrict staff access.
06 · Proposed integration design

Build one product-specific, read-only connector first

The safe first implementation is a narrow adapter for one approved API product and one customer-sponsored workflow. Use the exact Etimad API contract and authentication mechanism shown for that product; do not assume that every Etimad API uses the same operations, tokens, or data model.

LayerAURA implementationDesign decision / control
Customer entitlementTenant-level product configuration records customer, approved product, permitted purpose, subscription state, approved users, and data scope.Fail closed if the product is not enabled or the user does not have the approved role.
Identity and authorityMap an employee or supplier identifier only where the product requires it and the customer/provider authorizes the match.Use explicit identifier type and minimum required fields; Contracts Plus expects the identifier type to be stated.
Credential serviceServer-side secret storage; per-tenant or per-customer credentials based on Etimad’s approved model.Never expose app keys/secrets to a client app; audit access and support rotation/revocation.
Etimad adapterCall the selected product’s published endpoint, schema, and error handling; normalize responses to a small AURA domain model.Keep product-specific logic isolated; do not imply API functionality beyond the contract.
Employee/app experienceRender only the minimum approved response with source and last-checked time; provide a clear retry/support state.No broad search, cross-tenant sharing, or actionable “status tracking” until that operation is documented and approved.
OperationsMonitor success, latency, provider errors, subscription expiry, and cost per call; retain trace IDs and redacted logs.Honor data retention terms; avoid storing full response payloads unless explicitly permitted.

Failure and reconciliation handling

  • Classify provider validation errors, not-found results, permission errors, throttling, and outages separately.
  • Do not automatically retry billed failed requests until the product’s charging and retry policy is understood.
  • For the published Contracts Plus rate plan, Etimad says fees apply to both successful and failed inquiries, with codes 200, 400, and 404.
  • Capture usage counts to reconcile AURA logs against the Etimad invoice.

Security and privacy controls

  • Enforce role and purpose checks before every call; record who initiated the request and why.
  • Use least-privilege service access, encryption in transit and at rest, secrets rotation, and audit logs.
  • Define user notice/consent or other legal authority with the customer and provider before processing employee data.
  • Do not use returned data for another purpose or customer without written permission.

Cost components to confirm

  • Etimad subscription/rate plan and any per-query or per-record charge.
  • Whether failed, duplicate, and retried requests are billable.
  • Environment and production access fees, if any.
  • Implementation, security review, UAT, support, monitoring, and ongoing change management.
  • Permitted caching and retention, which may affect operating cost and user experience.
Cost controlFor a per-inquiry product, design for user-triggered, justified requests; prevent repeated refreshes, apply carefully scoped caching only if the provider permits it, show a confirmation where appropriate, and expose monthly usage and spend to the customer administrator.

09 · Recommendation

Proceed with a gated product decision

Proceed now

Discovery and customer qualification

Confirm the sponsor, exact user journey, eligible entity, candidate product, permitted purpose, sandbox route, current rate plan, and data conditions.

Conditional next

One product sandbox pilot

Start only after Etimad confirms product fit and account eligibility. Keep the scope read-only and limited to one customer and one defined data object.

Hold

General Etimad connector / purchase tracking

Do not market Etimad as a general procurement API or commit to purchase-request tracking until the specific endpoint, access terms, operations, and fees are evidenced.

Board recommendationKeep Etimad in the Saudi connector portfolio at rank 12 / Wave D Discovery. Fund product validation, not production implementation. Promote to a build wave only when a named AURA customer confirms demand and Etimad approves the exact API product and intended use.
