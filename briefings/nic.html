01 · Executive summary

NIC offers a route into government services, subject to sponsor and service approval

Institution

NIC enables government infrastructure

SDAIA describes NIC as operating national data and identity infrastructure and government integration capabilities. GSB consumer onboarding has formal agreements, network connection and per-service authorization steps.

Project evidence

The guide is a gateway contract baseline

The 28 September 2026 Java guide lists eight HTTP operations across person/eligibility, permit/visitor and worker hosts. It is an implementation baseline, not proof that the endpoints are NIC public APIs or that AURA can call them.

Board decision

Do discovery before a build commitment

Ask a government or pilgrimage-sector customer to sponsor one named service. Confirm gateway owners, lawful purpose, credentials, sandbox, data catalog, pricing and production approvals.

Access constraintSDAIA’s GSN eligibility information allows a non-government company to connect to a government entity only with approval from that entity and the service provider. The GSB flow separately requires a linking agreement and service authorizations. Neither source confirms AURA eligibility or access to the three hosts in the internal guide. Official GSN/GSB access conditions.

Employee Hub value

Make a seasonal, high-friction permit journey easier to understand: explain prerequisites, capture employer-provided inputs, show accepted/rejected/pending, remind about expiry and route the employee to HR or the service owner.

Direct checks or permit actions only after AURA is explicitly authorized to act for the employer and the individual workflow is approved.

Customer value to prove

  • Reduce manual document chasing and duplicate data entry.
  • Shorten readiness checks before a Hajj assignment or site entry.
  • Reduce unresolved or expired permit cases through status visibility.
  • Measure operator time, error/rejection rates and employee support volume during one season.

Official consumer onboarding route

  1. Identify consumer entity and sign linking agreement.
  2. Connect through GSN or approved NIC network path.
  3. Install/configure GSBExpress in test and production as instructed.
  4. Submit the consumer linking and named service authorization request.
  5. Obtain credentials and validate the approved contract in test.

SDAIA’s page describes this for the Government Service Bus. Determine with the sponsoring entity whether it applies to the guide’s separate gateways.

Implementation controls from the guide

  • Keep TLS validation enabled; use owner-approved HTTPS in real environments.
  • Set connect/request timeouts; disable automatic redirects.
  • Do not blindly retry writes after timeout; record unknown state and reconcile.
  • Test exact HTTP methods, route casing, headers, field casing, and business outcomes with approved sandbox samples.
  • Use mock tests first; never use live permit or registration records for development.
06 · Operating model and data protection

Make identity and permit actions traceable and reversible

Workflow state

  • Separate transport failure, authorization denial, business rejection and accepted result.
  • For a timeout after quota/permit write, show “status unknown”; reconcile before resubmission.
  • Require human confirmation before quota update, permit creation or cancellation.
  • Retain external permit ID, transaction reference, request UUID/trace ID and timestamps.
  • Give authorized operators a queue for unresolved and expiring permits.

Data controls

  • Use least privilege and separate secrets per gateway; worker bearer token and app-id/app-key must not be reused.
  • Encrypt identity, passport, visa and worker data in transit and at rest.
  • Redact identifiers and payloads in logs; do not store images unless required and approved.
  • Set purpose-bound access, retention, deletion and incident procedures with the customer/service owner.
  • Log who viewed, changed, approved, exported or cancelled a record.
Schema cautionThe guide lists identity and permit fields, but it states that required-field matrices, catalogs, date rules and stable success codes require owner confirmation. Avoid hard-coding assumptions from structural examples.
