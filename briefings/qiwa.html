01 · What is Qiwa?

Saudi Arabia’s digital platform for labor-system services

Verified

Labor services

HRSD describes Qiwa as the main digital platform for Saudi labor-system services and transactions between employees, establishments and government.

Verified

Employment contracts

Establishments create and document contracts. Employees can approve, reject or request changes; when both parties agree, the contract is certified by HRSD. [1]

AURA scope

Connector

A governed employee-service entry point for selected contract and employment journeys, with status visibility only where Qiwa authorizes a supported interface.

System-of-record boundaryQiwa remains the official service and employment record. AURA can explain, guide and hand off; it must not imply a contract was approved, changed or terminated unless the official authorized workflow confirms it.
02 · Value for the employee digital hub

Make work-related services easier to find and complete

Qiwa is highly relevant to Saudi employee lifecycle tasks. The employee should review and decide inside the official service for contract actions. AURA can reduce navigation and status confusion if the employer and provider approve the data flow.

04 · How to integrate

Start with an official handoff, then build only on approved access

Employee
AURA guidance
Official Qiwa
Employee reviews
Qiwa confirms
AURA shows allowed status

Initial pattern. AURA provides guidance and a provider-approved service link; the employee signs in and completes the action in Qiwa. No Qiwa credentials or OTPs pass through AURA.

Implementation plan

Choose the journey

Start with contract review or employment certificate; name the employer, employee audience and service owner.

Confirm access and authority

Ask Qiwa/HRSD and the customer whether an external API/partner route exists, who may access it, and what consent/terms apply.

Prototype the approved route

Use service links first. If an approved API is supplied, validate only the specified operations and data fields in an authorized test environment.

Operate with controls

Minimize employment data, audit access, monitor status freshness, and route contract decisions to the official employee workflow.

Do not assume an open APINo public Qiwa developer contract or AURA-ready execution API was established in the sources reviewed. Do not scrape, automate the portal, reuse employee credentials, or select endpoints/authentication based on guesswork.
