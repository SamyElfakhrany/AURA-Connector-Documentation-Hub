01 · What is it?

A specialist visa-form execution contract

Official company

Tasheer

The Saudi Company for Visa and Travel Solutions operates overseas visa centers and offers visa, travel, identity and Hajj/Umrah solutions. [1]

Gateway contract

Tasheer Connect

The supplied guide defines four bearer-token write routes for a shared request number: submit form, update form, update email and hold request. [4]

Aura scope

Governed adapter

A backend component that validates an approved request, freezes the provider catalog version, submits it and stores the returned transaction and business state.

Scope boundaryTasheer’s public services include form support, appointments, biometrics, payments and application tracking. The supplied Connect contract covers form-state writes only. It does not document biometric capture, fee settlement, appointment booking, passport handling or visa approval decisions.
02 · Value for Aura

Simplify a sponsored international-mobility workflow

Best fit: employers, mobility teams or authorized travel specialists with an approved Tasheer relationship and repeat form-submission process. The value is back-office orchestration and status discipline—not replacing applicant consent, consular decisions or in-person steps.

04 · How to integrate

Pin the form catalog and preserve one external request identity

Define the journey

Name applicant type, country, visa/form code, authorized operator, in-person steps and expected completion state.

Obtain provider access

Receive approved URLs, token lifecycle, allowlisting, form/category/field catalog and success/rejection codes.

Prove submit

Test validation, accepted/rejected response, duplicate submission and lost-response reconciliation with synthetic data.

Add maintenance

Enable update, email change and hold only after authorization, state-transition and recovery rules are approved.

06 · Access, prerequisites & blockers

Not build-ready until the catalog, authority and recovery path are approved

Confirm before build

Prerequisites

  • Named Tasheer integration owner, eligible customer and written production entitlement.
  • Exact country, applicant, form/visa types and supported service-center workflow.
  • Sandbox/production URLs, bearer-token lifecycle and network/certificate requirements.
  • Versioned form, category, field, hold-reason and business-code catalogs.
  • Required/optional fields, validation, update rules and accepted/rejected examples.
  • Duplicate/reconciliation method, rate limits, SLA, incidents and commercial terms.
Delivery controls

Guardrails

  • Purpose- and role-check before every request; keep applicant consent where required.
  • Vault tokens and redact passports, identities, contact data and payload values from logs.
  • Pin the catalog version used for every submission.
  • Separate local approval, provider submission and provider acceptance states.
  • Never auto-retry writes; reconcile using request number and transaction reference.
  • Use synthetic sandbox records; retain only fields needed for service and support.

07 · Cost

No verified public Connect price

Applicant or service-center fees do not establish API pricing. The integration cost depends on partner onboarding, eligible countries/forms, volumes, environments, identity/data controls, support, and whether payments or other Tasheer services are contracted separately.

Provider access

Partner onboarding, environments, catalog/support access, transaction fees and SLA.

Compliance

Applicant consent, privacy/security assessment, cross-border/data-role review and audit.

Aura delivery

Discovery, mapping, adapter, approvals, durable state, reconciliation, tests, monitoring and support.

Total = Tasheer commercial rights/transaction fees + Aura implementation + privacy/security assurance + recurring monitoring and support
Quote requiredRequest a written proposal from the confirmed Tasheer owner for the exact customer, countries, forms, operations, volumes, data fields, environments, SLA and support. Do not use public visa-center fees as the connector estimate.

Board decision

Fund discovery only. Authorize a synthetic submit-form sandbox proof after provider eligibility, catalog, business codes, data governance and reconciliation are confirmed. Add update, email-change and hold operations only after their state-transition controls are approved.
