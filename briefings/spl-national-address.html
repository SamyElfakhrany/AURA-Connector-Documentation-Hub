01 · What is SPL?

Saudi Post | SPL and the National Address service

Verified

Provider

Saudi Post | SPL provides the National Address service and its official API portal.

Verified

Service

The APIs support address search, verification, geographic lookups, maps and related functions.

Aura scope

Connector

A server-side Aura integration that sends approved address inputs to SPL and returns a result for employee review.

Simple distinction Verification checks a structured address. Search finds possible addresses from partial input. Lookups provide standard region, city and district lists.

Proposed architecture. Credentials stay in the Aura backend. The employee confirms the result before it is saved to the approved HR or service system.

Implementation plan that enables the journey above

Confirm access

Register, select the SPL product, obtain credentials and confirm test/production rights.

Build the adapter

Call Verify an Address, one search option and the region/city/district lookups.

Map and review

Map building, postcode, additional number, street, district, city and region; show the result to the user.

Operate safely

Use secrets management, rate limits, safe logs, monitoring, retries for temporary failures and role-based access.

Initial scope only Include verification, assisted search and location lookups. Exclude maps, bulk search and Address By Phone/OTP. SPL explicitly describes the phone flow as private for specific customers.

Aura internal cost

Plan separately

Integration development, employee UX, QA, security/privacy review, monitoring and support.

Monthly cost formula SPL subscription + (billable calls × SPL unit rate) + Aura infrastructure + support. The formula cannot be converted into SAR until SPL supplies the commercial plan.

Board decision requested

Approve a limited pilot for employee-entered address verification, one assisted-search option and region/city/district lookups.

Production gate: written confirmation of SPL price, plan, quota, SLA, production rights, permitted use/retention and the customer data owner.
