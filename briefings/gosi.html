01 · Executive decision

GOSI is a meaningful Saudi HR connector with a real enterprise integration channel

The AURA workbook placed GOSI at rank 14, Wave D—Discovery, score 56/100, confidence Low. Current official GOSI material adds an important fact: it identifies a System-to-System channel for large companies, enabling electronic transactions between company systems and GOSI using digital certificates and electronic signatures. That raises the access-feasibility assessment, but does not prove AURA can onboard, use a specific API, or access a test environment.

Verified channel

System-to-System exists

Official GOSI page describes direct transactions for large companies with digital certificates and electronic signatures.

High product fit

Employer + employee journeys

GOSI services cover contributor registration, exclusion, wage records, lists, account information, certificates, contribution periods, and quick verification.

Access still gated

No AURA contract confirmed

Public research did not identify the current S2S API specification, AURA onboarding rules, sandbox credentials, service-level terms, or direct integration price.

RecommendationMove GOSI from generic discovery into a focused access-validation track. Start with one large Saudi employer sponsor and one narrowly defined HR/payroll flow. Keep AURA in discovery until GOSI confirms that the customer or AURA’s platform can use the supported S2S channel and provides the current technical and commercial pack.
02 · Product and service scope

GOSI serves both employer operations and contributor self-service

The product opportunity is broader than a simple “insurance status” lookup. GOSI publishes distinct employer, contributor, verification, payment, and system-to-system service channels. The employee and employer entitlements differ and should stay separated in AURA.

Why GOSI fits Employee Hub

  • Saudi employee insurance status and contribution-period information are core work-life records.
  • AURA can connect onboarding, HR profile changes, payroll tasks, employee requests and compliance follow-up around one employee lifecycle.
  • The best employee-facing experience may initially be a trusted handoff to the employee’s own GOSI/Taminaty account.

Why the first direct build should be employer-led

  • The documented S2S channel is explicitly described for large companies and direct company-system transactions.
  • Employer operations and payroll updates have a clear customer owner and business-process sponsor.
  • Employee self-service is a distinct access context; do not reuse an employer certificate to read a worker’s personal account.
Do not claim before approvalAutomatic employee-status sync, full contribution-history retrieval, GOSI registration or wage updates from AURA, payment initiation, or status callbacks. Each requires a verified S2S operation, customer authority, exact permissions, sandbox test, and production approval.

05 · Access and onboarding

There is an S2S channel, but its integration contract is not public

Officially documented

  • GOSI describes a System-to-System channel for large companies to conduct electronic transactions directly between their systems and GOSI.
  • GOSI states that the channel uses digital certificates and electronic signatures.
  • Employer services are tied to the registered establishment and its authorized employer/supervisor roles.
  • Public employer service pages show some web services as free, including adding contributors and issuing a contributor list.
  • GOSI’s public e-service catalog gives service descriptions, user steps, target audience, channels and, for some services, response times.

Not confirmed for AURA

  • Whether the 2026 S2S channel is open to an HR software provider such as AURA, or only to the employer’s own systems / approved integrators.
  • Current API/transport spec, message schema, endpoints, certificate issuance, signing algorithm, identity registration and network requirements.
  • Sandbox/test tenant, test records, certification or approval process, production cutover and rate limits.
  • Whether the intended contributor, wage, list, status, certificate or payment operations are available over S2S.
  • Provider/partner fees, support SLA, change notice, data retention and production eligibility.
1 · SponsorName one Saudi employer, GOSI establishment and owner of the workflow.
2 · QualifyAsk GOSI whether employer or AURA can use S2S for the exact transaction.
3 · Obtain packRequest contract, interface specs, certificates, sandbox, test identities and pricing.
4 · ValidateTest one workflow including rejection, duplicate and uncertain outcomes.
5 · ApproveSecurity, legal, customer and GOSI approve production before go-live.
Important distinction“GOSI supports System-to-System integration” is verified. “AURA has access to GOSI APIs” is not verified. A public web service’s free fee does not determine the price or terms for enterprise machine access.
06 · Proposed integration design

Start with a customer-owned, certificate-based adapter

If GOSI approves a third-party integration, implement one customer-specific adapter around the exact S2S contract. The published evidence mentions digital certificates and e-signatures, but does not provide enough detail to choose a protocol, key format, or API style.

LayerProposed AURA responsibilityControl / open design question
Customer and entitlementRegister the establishment, enabled GOSI operations, customer administrator, business owner, and approved AURA tenant.Get written confirmation of employer-owned versus AURA/integrator-owned access and any per-tenant certificate requirement.
Identity and authorizationMap AURA employee IDs to GOSI contributor identity only where permitted; keep employer users and employee self-service contexts separate.Confirm identifier types, sponsor/establishment linkage, role matrix and employee authorization model.
Secure transportUse provider-issued or provider-approved digital certificate/signature mechanism; store private keys in a managed secrets/HSM service.Follow the actual GOSI technical pack for certificate issuance, signing, TLS, rotation, timestamping and revocation.
Workflow adapterBegin with one operation family, such as contributor status reconciliation or employee changes. Normalize approved provider response into AURA status and case records.Do not assume REST, SOAP, file/batch, webhook, or synchronous behavior until the contract confirms it.
Human approvalShow a preview and require authorized HR/payroll confirmation before writes to contributor registration, exclusion or wages.Which operations can be automated, delegated or require evidence attachments / approval by employer?
ReconciliationPersist local operation ID, GOSI request/reference, state, timestamps and redacted result; expose pending/rejected/accepted outcomes to an authorized admin.Confirm provider idempotency, query/retry model, file result semantics and reconciliation SLA.
Employee experienceFor personal certificates or contribution history, use official GOSI/Taminaty handoff until an employee-authorized API is approved.Never display employer data to an employee or employee personal history to an employer without explicit entitlement.

Operational handling

  • Separate validation/business rejection from network failure and unknown transaction outcomes.
  • Do not blindly replay create/update operations after a timeout; reconcile through the owner-approved query mechanism first.
  • Show the GOSI request reference and last update time in AURA and provide an exception queue for unresolved items.
  • Monitor certificate expiry, request errors, file/batch results, latency and per-customer usage.

Data protection

  • Limit each call to the defined business purpose and minimum employee fields.
  • Encrypt personal identifiers, wage and contribution data; avoid full payloads in application logs.
  • Enforce tenant isolation and role-based access with audit history for view, export and change actions.
  • Set retention, deletion, correction and employee-support processes with the customer and GOSI.
