01 · Executive decision

Confirm the invoice issuer before choosing the connection route

ZATCA operates the Saudi e-invoicing framework. The project drive also contains a Java guide for a separate `/billing/v3` intermediary gateway. That guide documents tax-profile setup, company/entity setup, paid-invoice submission, invoice detail lookup and download; it explicitly says the routes are the intermediary’s contract, not direct ZATCA endpoints.

Verified official route

Direct Fatoora

ZATCA publishes XML, data dictionary, security, onboarding, API, clearance and reporting material for in-scope taxpayers and compliant EGS solutions.

Existing project route

Intermediary `/billing/v3`

An internal guide provides a Java contract baseline for a gateway invoice journey. Its owner, production access, sandbox credentials and AURA commercial rights remain unverified.

First business question

Who is the seller?

Identify who supplies the paid employee service, receives payment and must issue the invoice: AURA, the employer/customer or another merchant.

RecommendationApprove a short issuer-and-access discovery, then pilot one paid service under one named taxpayer. Reuse the intermediary route only with owner-issued access and a confirmed contract. Choose direct Fatoora only where the actual invoice issuer and EGS are eligible and onboarded. Do not treat this connector as a payment gateway.
02 · Product and regulatory scope

ZATCA sets the invoice rules; the seller’s EGS generates and submits

ZATCA’s public guidance distinguishes Phase 1 invoice generation from Phase 2 integration. Phase 2 applies in waves to targeted taxpayer groups. For an integrated EGS, Fatoora APIs handle the relevant clearance/reporting interaction; tax invoice creation, VAT treatment, payment collection and buyer presentment remain separate responsibilities and decisions.

What the connector does

Connect an AURA billing or finance workflow to the taxpayer’s approved invoice-generation/compliance route; associate the invoice with a verified business transaction; expose invoice state or artifact to the right user.

What it does not do

Collect card payments, decide who is merchant of record, determine VAT treatment, make a non-taxpayer subject to Phase 2, or convert an intermediary contract into direct ZATCA access. Payment collection belongs to the payment provider; tax ownership belongs to the seller.

06 · Proposed integration design

Keep payment success, invoice acceptance and fulfillment as separate states

AURA should maintain independent business states for payment, tax invoice processing and service fulfillment. Never treat an HTTP 2xx response alone as proof that payment settled or ZATCA accepted an invoice.

Route A · Existing `/billing/v3` gateway

  1. Obtain owner-approved gateway base URL, credentials, sandbox, allowlist and tenant scope.
  2. Complete tax profile and company/entity/service setup as required by the gateway flow.
  3. Verify the payment with the payment provider/merchant system; persist payment reference and status.
  4. Submit the paid invoice to the documented gateway operation; validate business response and store invoice reference.
  5. Retrieve invoice details/download through gateway routes; validate content type and document integrity.

Project guide routes include profile/company/entity setup, POST /billing/v3/api/invoice/paid, POST /billing/v3/api/invoice/invoicedetails/{invoiceNumber}, and POST /billing/v3/api/invoice/download. Treat these as gateway routes only.

Route B · Direct Fatoora EGS

  1. Confirm issuer/taxpayer is in scope and identify the Phase 2 wave and document types.
  2. Use a compliant EGS to generate required XML/business fields and security features.
  3. Onboard the EGS and obtain the applicable credentials/certificate identifiers through the official process.
  4. Send standard B2B documents through Clearance before buyer presentment; send simplified B2C documents via Reporting within the stated timeframe.
  5. Store ZATCA response, clearance/reporting state, warnings/errors, UUID/hash/counter/reference and buyer document copy as allowed.

Use current ZATCA technical resources. The SDK helps test compliance; it does not approve the EGS or relieve the seller of compliance responsibility.

10 · Board recommendation

Approve discovery and a narrow intermediary pilot, not a broad tax connector

Proceed now

Issuer and contract validation

Identify the seller and ask the `/billing/v3` owner for its current contract, sandbox, price, credentials and AURA/customer-use approval.

Pilot option

One paid-service invoice

Run a single end-to-end flow only after payment and invoice responsibilities are clear; reconcile gateway response, invoice artifact and service fulfillment.

Hold

Direct Fatoora productization

Keep separate until an in-scope taxpayer, EGS model, technical owner, onboarding path and funding are named.

Portfolio conclusionKeep ZATCA Integration Service in Wave D—Discovery. The internal intermediary guide creates a practical pilot option, while official ZATCA documentation makes the direct route technically understandable. Access and commercial rights are still separate gates for each route.
