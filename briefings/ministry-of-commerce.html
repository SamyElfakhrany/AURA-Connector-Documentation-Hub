01 · Executive summary

Commercial registration checks can benefit multiple AURA apps, if transactional access is approved

Official services

The Ministry offers CR services online

Public Ministry pages document commercial registration services through the Saudi Business Center and CR-data inquiry. Some journeys require an authenticated beneficiary and/or a verification code.

Project guide

Four routes are documented internally

The 28 September Java guide lists basic CR information, related-person-to-CR information, detailed CR data and an Umrah CR status notification route. These are technical contract candidates, not verified public Ministry APIs.

Access gap

Public CR statistics API is a different product

The Ministry publishes an API guide for CR statistical/GIS information. That does not prove access to personal CR details, owner relationships, or the notification endpoint described in the project guide.

Board conclusionKeep the connector at 44/100 and Wave D—Discovery. No official evidence reviewed connects AURA to the guide’s transactional routes. Verify the service owner, approved purpose, customer entitlement, contract, sandbox and production terms before development.
02 · Purpose and customer use cases

Use Ministry connectivity for business identity, not general employee verification

The strongest cross-app opportunity is to validate a Saudi business record during onboarding or a business transaction. Employee Hub has a supporting role when an authorized employee or delegate handles an employer’s commercial service.

Employee experience value

Make the correct corporate service discoverable, show prerequisites and clarify who in the company is allowed to complete it. For direct integrations, show the status, source and time so employees can see whether a record needs attention.

This is primarily a business-entity connector. It does not provide general employee personal data or employee identity verification.

Value to measure

  • Time to validate new customers, suppliers or business partners.
  • Manual checks and duplicate data entry removed.
  • Incorrect or stale CR records caught before an app workflow proceeds.
  • Review workload for inactive, mismatched or inconclusive results.
  • Number and timeliness of acknowledged Umrah status notices, if in scope.

04 · Product and operation map

Keep the four operations, token scopes and business decisions distinct

Service groupGuide operationRequestCredential in guideProduct control
Basic CR informationPOST /CRbasicInfoCRInqueryRequest; CR number and version contextBearer BASIC_TOKENValidate provider business response and CR state; HTTP 2xx alone is not acceptance.
Person-related CRPOST /PersonRelatedCRCRPersonalInfoRequest; identifier type/number and nationalityBearer PERSONAL_TOKENOnly call for an approved identity/relationship purpose. Do not infer signing authority from a nonempty result.
Detailed CR informationPOST /crdetailsCRInqueryRequestBearer BASIC_TOKENRequest minimum profile; confirm which fields may be stored, displayed and refreshed.
CR status notificationPOST /UpdateCRStatusMocNotifyRequest; CRNumber and StatusID, plus source/time/message headersBearer NOTIFICATION_TOKENSpecific notification operation; persist acknowledgement and delivery state; do not treat it as an unrestricted status-update right.
Important distinctionThe Ministry’s public “CR REST API” guide is described as retrieving CR information for statistical/GIS purposes. The internal Java guide’s transactional routes, including person-related and status notification calls, need their own service owner and approval. Do not use the public stats API as evidence for these routes.

Possible response model

  • Verified: provider response meets the approved business success rule.
  • Needs review: data mismatch or relationship result needs human confirmation.
  • Not verified: inactive/missing CR or authorized validation fails.
  • Unavailable: timeout, outage, authorization error or malformed response.

Do not overstate

  • No general “company is approved” conclusion from a basic CR response.
  • No assumption that a related person can sign, delegate or act for the company.
  • No auto-block on inconclusive third-party or stale data.
  • No status notification to a provider without a defined contractual purpose.

Suggested first end-to-end flow

  1. Customer starts supplier or tenant onboarding and provides a CR number.
  2. AURA calls only the approved basic CR query.
  3. AURA maps provider status to verified, review or unable-to-verify.
  4. AURA displays minimum fields and timestamp, with manual correction/escalation.
  5. AURA records a redacted audit event and provider reference.

Failure and retry behavior

  • Use TLS verification, bounded timeout and no blind redirect.
  • Differentiate transport failure from a business rejection returned with HTTP 200.
  • On timeout after notification, persist an unknown/pending outcome and reconcile before resending.
  • Use bounded backoff only for safe, owner-approved operations.
  • Run mock tests first, then owner-approved sandbox test cases.
06 · Operating model and data protection

Limit CR and personal data to the business purpose that justified the call

Service governance

  • Maintain separate service scopes and credentials for basic, personal and notification operations.
  • Apply tenant and purpose checks before every lookup; do not allow bulk enumeration without explicit approval.
  • Set an owner-approved rule for mismatch, inactive CR, owner mismatch and not-found results.
  • Give data owners a way to correct or dispute stale business information.
  • Review authorized use and customer delegation regularly.

Security and data handling

  • Use least privilege, secret rotation and a server-side integration layer.
  • Encrypt identifiers and provider payloads in transit and at rest.
  • Keep personal identifiers and CR payloads out of routine logs and analytics.
  • Define display, retention, deletion, export and support rules with the customer and service owner.
  • Audit who initiated, viewed, approved or retried each lookup/notification.
Guide limitsThe guide calls for owner-approved sample requests, field catalogs, required-field rules, stable success codes, sandbox and production URLs. These are not established by its structural schemas or the public statistics API.
