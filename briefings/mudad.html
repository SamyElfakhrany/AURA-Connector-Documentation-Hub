01 · Executive summary

Mudad is a high-value payroll experience; access is the unresolved dependency

Product evidence

Mudad joins payroll and compliance work

Mudad describes payroll transfers, WPS compliance, establishment bank linking, user delegation, proactive violation checks and reports. HRSD describes the WPS cycle, including employer explanations and employee accept/reject actions.

Integration evidence

There are partner routes

Al Rajhi documents automated WPS file submission from its payroll service to Mudad. This is a bank-to-Mudad route for eligible bank customers, not proof of direct AURA API access. The bank remains an intermediary and the employer remains responsible for payroll-file accuracy.

Access gap

No general Mudad developer API verified

The connector register marks direct API access unverified. No Mudad-specific technical guide or AURA credentials were found in the project material reviewed. Any direct API, webhook, SSO, data export or partner arrangement must be confirmed with the owner.

Board conclusionKeep Mudad at 44/100 and Wave D—Discovery. Proceed with Employee Hub service design and official handoff. Fund direct system integration only after Mudad or an approved payroll-bank partner confirms the interface, customer entitlement, fields, sandbox and commercial terms.
02 · Purpose and customer use cases

Connect the employee wage journey with employer payroll operations

Mudad can support both sides of wage protection: an employer prepares and submits wage information and explains exceptions; an employee can review an explanation and accept or reject it in Mudad. AURA can make that journey easier to understand and track, subject to an approved integration model.

Recommended first Employee Hub flow

  1. Employee sees a wage-exception notification or support case from the employer’s HR/payroll system.
  2. AURA explains what action is needed and routes employee to Mudad account.
  3. Employee reviews employer explanation and accepts or rejects within Mudad.
  4. Employee returns to AURA support/checklist; HR follows up through its authorized employer view.
  5. If approved status integration later exists, AURA updates case state with source and timestamp.

Integration controls

  • Never put Mudad, bank or payroll credentials in the client/browser.
  • Use tenant-bound employer and individual permissions.
  • Keep employee wage data separate from manager access and peer visibility.
  • Represent pending, accepted, rejected, delayed and unknown outcomes distinctly.
  • Reconcile payroll transfers and WPS acceptance; a successful bank transfer does not itself prove WPS acceptance.
06 · Operating model and data protection

Protect payroll data and keep responsibility with the authorized employer

Operational governance

  • Identify employer, payroll processor, bank, Mudad account owner and employee roles.
  • Keep payroll approval and salary transfer under employer/bank authorization; AURA is not the merchant or payroll operator by default.
  • Maintain separate states for bank transfer, WPS file delivery, Mudad processing and employee response.
  • Define manual fallback if a bank upload fails or Mudad rejects a file.
  • Keep an auditable case trail with provider/bank reference and event timestamps.

Data protection

  • Minimize salary, national ID, bank and adjustment fields to the selected journey.
  • Encrypt payloads in transit and at rest; redact logs and analytics.
  • Restrict employee view to their own records; scope HR/payroll access by employer role.
  • Define payroll retention, correction, export and employee-support procedures.
  • Never infer employee agreement from lack of response or a bank payout event.
Legal and compliance boundaryHRSD identifies itself as the regulator for the Wage Protection Program. The employer remains responsible for WPS compliance and accurate payroll data. Confirm the applicable rules and customer responsibilities with HRSD and legal/compliance owners before turning a workflow into automated decisions.
