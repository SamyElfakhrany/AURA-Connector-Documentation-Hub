01 · Executive summary

There is a bookable hotel workflow, but the gateway supplier is unidentified

Project evidence

Five booking operations are described

The internal guide provides POST routes for search, details, reservation, system cancellation and modification. It describes request/response schemas, business status handling, hotel/rate references and booking references.

Market context

Nusuk is a distinct official channel

The Ministry-supervised Nusuk Umrah platform offers packages from approved service providers, including accommodation. This confirms an official market route, but not that Nusuk operates the project’s CRS gateway.

Critical gap

No owner, auth or access terms verified

The guide says to obtain access and credentials from the gateway owner but defines no explicit authentication header. The connector register marks the vendor, commercial rights and AURA entitlement unresolved.

Board conclusionKeep CRS at 37/100 and Wave D—Discovery. Public Umrah booking evidence is useful market context, not proof of this supplier or a CRS integration entitlement. First confirm the legal vendor, gateway owner, customer account, booking terms and test environment.

Recommended first workflow

  1. Choose one customer and identify the CRS legal supplier.
  2. Choose one use case: employee travel or licensed Umrah group accommodation.
  3. Search, load details and confirm rate/availability.
  4. Require customer approval before reservation.
  5. Store provider reference and verify change/cancel outcomes in sandbox.

Failure and reconciliation

  • If create times out, check booking status by provider reference/correlation before retrying.
  • Do not treat HTTP 200 alone as business success; guide expects status.code == “0” and a non-null response.
  • For cancellation/modify timeout, preserve “outcome unknown” until reconciled.
  • Keep hotel inventory/rates time-stamped; avoid promising stale quotes.
  • Set a manual travel-operations fallback for urgent trips.
06 · Operating model and traveler safeguards

Make booking terms, personal data and supplier responsibility visible

Booking operations

  • Identify the party responsible for itinerary, traveler support, supplier payment and booking changes.
  • Store the booking reference, property, dates, room/guest count, price snapshot and cancellation rules.
  • Separate requested, pending, confirmed, modified, cancelled, failed and unknown states.
  • Confirm occupancy, name matching, arrival/check-in rules and group permit constraints with the CRS owner.
  • Reconcile bookings and supplier invoices with the customer’s travel/finance process.

Traveler data protection

  • Collect only the traveler/guest information required by the approved booking flow.
  • Restrict group rosters and guest passport/identity data to authorized travel staff.
  • Encrypt sensitive details, mask logs and set retention/deletion rules.
  • Make cancellation, modification and support terms available before confirmation.
  • Separate employee self-service from travel desk and Umrah service-provider administration roles.
Official-channel contextNusuk Umrah currently presents packages with accommodation from Ministry-approved service providers. AURA should decide whether it is serving a licensed provider’s back-office workflow, a customer’s employee travel flow, or simply linking to Nusuk; avoid duplicating listings or booking without supplier rights.
