01 · What is it?

Rail fulfilment through an intermediary contract

Official service

SAR

Saudi Arabia Railways offers passenger journeys, schedules, web/app ticket purchase and booking management. [1] [2]

Integration layer

Masar gateway

The project guide describes Basic authentication plus a party-key and eight shared routes for search, booking, payment, inquiry, cancellation and contract requests. [4]

Aura scope

Controlled adapter

A server-side connector that applies policy, sends the approved operation, stores provider references and reconciles uncertain outcomes.

Do not collapse the partiesThe supplied document explicitly says its contract is for an intermediary, not a direct railway endpoint. Public SAR pages confirm passenger services, but they do not establish Masar’s identity, commercial authority or Aura’s entitlement.

04 · How to integrate

Validate the gateway contract before building the write path

Verify authority

Name the gateway owner, contracting entity, SAR relationship, permitted journeys, support owner and data roles.

Obtain sandbox

Freeze the contract

Confirm required fields, catalogs, date/timezone, currency, business codes, size/rate limits and reconciliation rules.

Prove operations

Validate every operation with accepted sandbox requests/responses, security tests and support evidence.

Response and reliability contract from the supplied guide

Treat HTTP success and business success separately. The guide describes a wrapper with responseCode, data, responseDesc, traceError and requestUUID; string code "0" plus non-null data is the stated success condition. Persist a local operation ID, business reference, provider reference, request fingerprint, operation, state, attempt count and timestamps. Safe reads may use bounded retry; writes require inquiry/reconciliation before resubmission after an ambiguous timeout.

06 · Access, prerequisites & blockers

Not build-ready until authority, sandbox and operating rules are evidenced

Confirm before build

Commercial and technical prerequisites

  • Legal identity of the gateway owner and written authority for the intended SAR service.
  • Sandbox and production base URLs, credentials, party-key, rotation and network controls.
  • Required-field matrix, station/train/fare catalogs, passenger rules, dates, timezone and currency.
  • Booking, payment, cancellation, refund, settlement and duplicate-handling rules.
  • Rate/size limits, uptime/SLA, incident escalation and support contacts.
  • Passenger-data controller/processor roles, retention, privacy and security approval.
Delivery guardrails

Recommended controls

  • Release search and inquiry before any transactional operation.
  • Require explicit user approval immediately before booking, payment or cancellation.
  • Keep secrets in a vault; redact credentials, documents and payment data from logs.
  • Persist booking, payment, cancellation and contract-payment states separately.
  • Never auto-retry writes; mark unknown and reconcile using saved references.
  • Minimize passenger PII and test only with approved synthetic sandbox data.

07 · Cost

No verified public gateway price

Passenger fares published by SAR are not connector pricing. The integration cost depends on the intermediary agreement, permitted journeys, transaction volume, payment/settlement model, environments, support and Aura delivery scope.

Provider rights

Gateway onboarding, SAR service entitlement, sandbox/production access, per-booking charges and SLA.

Payments

Payment processing, settlement, cancellation/refund handling and financial reconciliation.

Aura delivery

Discovery, security review, adapter build, tests, monitoring, privacy controls and ongoing support.

Total = gateway/SAR commercial rights + transaction/payment fees + Aura implementation + security/privacy work + recurring monitoring and support
Quote requiredRequest a written proposal from the verified contracting party covering environments, operations, volumes, payment flows, data roles, SLA/support and all recurring fees. Do not use public passenger ticket prices as an API estimate.

Board decision

Fund discovery only. Authorize a search-and-inquiry sandbox pilot after the intermediary’s identity, SAR authority, commercial model, credentials and accepted test contract are verified. Gate booking and payment behind a separate control review.
