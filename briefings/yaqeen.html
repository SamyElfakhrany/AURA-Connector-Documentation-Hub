01 · Executive decision

Qualify a limited verification use case before building a shared connector

Elm describes Yaqeen as a service for eligible organizations to verify data about people with whom they have an official relationship, including employees, applicants, customers and auditors. It connects electronically to official records and offers customer-specific subscription packages. Its page places limits around which information may be accessed for privacy reasons.

Verified product scope

Organization-led data verification

Useful where an eligible organization needs to check selected identity information against authorized official records as part of a defined relationship and process.

Best first use case

Applicant or employee onboarding

Validate a narrow set of employee identity attributes at an approved onboarding step, flag exceptions for HR review and keep a traceable verification result.

Keep gated

Broad, repeated or automated checks

Do not enable generic searches, bulk lookups, automated rejection, or continuous change alerts until purpose, permissions, fields, package and operating rules are confirmed.

RecommendationStart provider/customer discovery now. Build a customer-scoped connector only after Elm confirms AURA’s integration eligibility, the customer’s permitted purpose and exact fields, the technical contract, a test route and commercial terms. Offer guided handoff or human verification steps while those gates remain open.
02 · Product and service scope

Verify selected identity data for a known, authorized relationship

This report treats “Yaqeen” as Elm’s identity-data verification service listed in AURA’s connector register. It does not treat the name as a generic open API or as a single connector that automatically includes adjacent Elm products.

CapabilityOfficial product descriptionPotential AURA roleEvidence limit

Official-record linkageElm describes an electronic link with National Information Center and Saudi Post data in real time, within permitted privacy limits.Use provider-returned result to flag verified, mismatch, incomplete or review-needed records—depending on the actual response schema.Do not promise specific data coverage, source precedence, freshness SLA or exact response statuses until in the contract.
Other verification domainsElm’s service profile also lists person, vehicle and dependent-data verification as service areas.Potential later workflows where the customer is entitled to verify the related record and package includes the relevant operation.Each domain may have separate eligibility, purpose rules, data fields, usage and fees.
SubscriptionElm says Yaqeen is subscribed through packages suited to customer needs. The product page provides an organizational enquiry/subscription route.Connect a customer’s subscribed service to one or more AURA applications under the approved tenant model.Public pages do not disclose current price bands, package quotas, API fees or AURA’s integrator rights.
Data quality and fraud reductionElm positions Yaqeen as supporting more current, consistent official-record data and reduced fraudulent operations.Improve review quality and reduce manual rekeying in eligible verification steps.Yaqeen must not be treated as a fraud score or an automatic hiring/eligibility decision unless separately authorized and validated.

Yaqeen, Nafath and Natheer are different

Yaqeen: organization-led verification of permitted person data. Nafath: digital identity/authentication for access to service-provider journeys. Natheer: a separate monitoring service that tracks changes for an enrolled list. Keep each product, contract, user purpose and connector separate.

Use it across AURA applications

A common connector could support HR onboarding, applicant screening, employee record correction and other customer workflows that require an authorized identity check. Reuse is conditional on each customer’s eligibility and purpose; a successful subscription does not make every AURA app or business process entitled to query every field.

05 · Access and onboarding

Organization access exists; AURA’s specific contract and API pack do not

Officially documented

  • Yaqeen is offered to financial, public-service and other organizations, including verification of employees and applicants who have an official relationship with them.
  • Elm describes electronic linkage with National Information Center and Saudi Post records.

06 · Proposed integration design

Make purpose, minimum fields and human review part of the connector

The design below is a proposal for an Elm-approved interface. It does not assume REST, SOAP, an API key, or a specific authentication pattern. Use only the protocol and result contract supplied by Elm.

LayerAURA responsibilityProvider/customer confirmation
Customer and purposeRegister approved customer entity, business relationship, purpose code, user roles and allowed AURA product surfaces.Which entity subscribes and is controller/owner? Which exact purposes and data sources are approved?
Consent/notice and legal basisPresent the customer’s approved notice at the correct workflow step; store evidence of notice or authorization where required.Clarify which lawful basis, customer obligations, user notice and subject rights apply. Do not assume employment alone is sufficient.
Credential boundaryKeep provider credentials in a managed secret store, isolated per customer; audit administrative changes and rotate credentials per contract.Use Elm’s actual authentication, certificate, network and key lifecycle requirements.
Request minimizationSend only the minimum approved identifiers and attributes needed to run the requested verification.Confirm field-by-field entitlement, validation rules, data source meaning, limits, purpose restrictions and whether batch checks are allowed.
Result normalizationMap provider response into AURA states such as verified, mismatch, incomplete, pending or provider error only where schema supports them.Get exact response codes, match thresholds, timestamp semantics, error definitions and source-of-truth rules.
Decision and reviewDisplay only the permitted result to the authorized reviewer; route mismatch to a human correction process and provide a clear case reference.Confirm whether raw returned attributes can be displayed, stored, exported or used in downstream decisions.
Retention and auditRetain minimum evidence: purpose, actor, time, request reference, outcome and permitted fields; suppress raw identity payloads from logs.Set retention/deletion, correction, audit, incident notification, data residency and support responsibilities in writing.

Illustrative onboarding journey

  1. Applicant enters identity details in the customer’s AURA workflow.
  2. Before a check, AURA shows the customer-approved notice and validates eligibility/purpose.
  3. Authorized backend calls the Elm-approved Yaqeen operation and receives a result.
  4. AURA shows the approved result to HR; mismatches go to review/correction.
  5. AURA stores a minimal audit reference and does not treat an unresolved check as an adverse decision.

Platform controls

  • Tenant-specific entitlements and role-based access.
  • Idempotency and bounded retries per actual API contract; reconcile uncertain outcomes before replay.
  • Rate/usage meters by customer and package; alert on unusual query volume.
  • Clear “verified at” time, source label, result explanation and human escalation route.

10 · Board recommendation

Fund access discovery and one onboarding verification pilot

Proceed now

Provider qualification

Ask Elm to confirm AURA’s role, eligible customer profiles, allowed fields, purpose limits, test access, current package and rates.

Pilot option

HR onboarding verification

Validate one approved person-data check in a Saudi employer workflow; return a minimal result to HR with human review and correction handling.

Hold

General identity API or automated decisions

Do not market universal identity lookup, authentication, continuous record monitoring or automated hiring decisions as Yaqeen functionality.

Portfolio conclusionYaqeen is a potentially reusable verification component for qualified customer workflows, particularly onboarding. Keep the connector in Wave D—Discovery until access, scope, price and technical readiness are evidenced. Employee Hub should provide a guided, transparent verification journey and show results only where the employer and Elm authorize that use.
