AURA × Ajeer
Temporary-work and permit journey

Ajeer is an HRSD program for regulated temporary work and workforce access. Its Tasharik service is explicitly designed for electronic platforms/apps that use workforce supplied by operating companies through Ajeer permits. This is a strong partnership lead for AURA, while public evidence does not establish an AURA API or AURA eligibility.

01 · Executive summary

Ajeer has a direct platform partnership concept, but the published journey is portal-based

Official service

Tasharik targets apps

Ajeer says Tasharik lets electronic platforms/apps use workforce from operating companies through Ajeer permit issuance. The workflow uses establishment accounts, a contract offer, acceptance by the other party and then permit issuance.

Employee experience

Permit status can anchor a workforce journey

AURA Employee Hub can guide the worker through offer/assignment, required data, permit status, expiry and support. Employer/admin tools can track agreements and exceptions.

Integration gap

No external API is confirmed

The connector register and reviewed project-folder material do not contain an Ajeer API guide, endpoint, credential model, approved AURA integration or sandbox. The official service page describes a web workflow, not partner API rights.

Board conclusionKeep Ajeer in Wave D—Discovery. Reassess the baseline from 41 to 46/100 because a platform-specific product and a defined employer/contract/permit route are publicly documented. Do not treat this as proof that AURA is an eligible Tasharik platform or has machine-to-machine access.

Employee Hub value

Give seasonal and temporary workers one place to see what is expected, which party needs to act next, permit validity, assignment dates, required payment and how to get help. This is especially useful when a worker’s assignment is time-bound.

AURA should display only worker-specific data that the employer and Ajeer approve.

Value to measure

  • Time from contract offer to acceptance and permit issuance.
  • Worker assignments delayed for missing or expired permits.
  • Employer effort spent chasing workers, providers and approvals.
  • Seasonal vacancies filled through compliant temporary arrangements.
  • Permit fee/payment exceptions, expiries and support contacts.

Qualify AURA as a platform participant before designing an API adapter

First end-to-end workflow

  1. Employer chooses a program and verifies eligibility in Ajeer.
  2. Provider/beneficiary parties create and accept the applicable contract.
  3. Authorized party submits permit details and selected worker.
  4. Worker sees assignment details, required actions, payment step and status in Employee Hub via handoff/manual confirmation.
  5. Employer confirms issued permit and assignment start; AURA records source and timestamp.

Integration controls

  • No browser scraping, shared login or automated account actions without provider approval.
  • Keep Ajeer permit, employment contract, employer HR record and work assignment as separate objects.
  • Use explicit states for contract pending/accepted, payment pending, permit issued/rejected/expired.
  • Do not state “authorized to work” based only on contract acceptance or payment; require authoritative permit outcome.
  • Audit who initiated, approved, paid and confirmed each step.
06 · Operating model and worker safeguards

Keep the permit tied to the right worker, customer, service and time period

Operational governance

  • Use a service-specific checklist and eligibility rules approved by Ajeer/customer, not a single generic permit form.
  • Track provider, beneficiary establishment, worker, assignment, contract dates, permit number and expiry.
  • Block assignment readiness only on an authoritative, current permit status; route exceptions to HR/legal operations.
  • Provide seasonal cut-off reminders, pending-party queues and manual fallback.
  • Monitor service availability and support contacts ahead of seasonal peaks.

Employee data and fairness

  • Minimize identity and employment fields; limit access to the worker, assigned employer and authorized provider.
  • Do not expose another worker’s personal details, offer terms or documents.
  • Show who owns the next action and where official status came from.
  • Make assignment dates, fees and employer identity clear before acceptance.
  • Provide correction and escalation routes for incorrect identity or permit status.
Regulatory boundaryAjeer services implement temporary-work arrangements under applicable HRSD rules. AURA should not create legal eligibility decisions or replace provider/employer approvals; confirm requirements for each service with Ajeer and the participating customer.
